💡 Our tips
  • Use Metro station to reach all main sightseeing.
  • Enjoy walking in Rome.
  • Use buses in locations where metro is not available
  • Use Apps for itineraries : we like to use: citymapper.com
  • In touristic spots and in public transports look carefully after your goods at anytime. Pickpockets adore tourists.
🎟️ Ticketing
  • "Tap & Go" service: you pay instantly at turnstiles or on board with your contactless credit card. No extra fees. Remember each traveler must use a separate card.
  • Paper Tickets: Available at self-service machines in any Metro station.
🚇 Metro / Bus
  • Metro: Cipro Station, Line A (500m). This is the fastest and most cost-effective option.
  • Bus: Slower than the train, but more extensive coverage across territory.
    Tip: Line 492 goes through the whole city center, starting from Cipro
🚕 Taxi
  • General Dispatch: +39 06.06.09 (Connects to all local companies)
  • Recommended Private Line: +39 06.35.70 (Known for good standards).
  • Uber/apps: Usually limited to luxury "limousine" services.
  • Fares: Always verify the estimated fare with the driver.
  • Availability: During peak hours, expect long wait time for booking.
🅿️ Car parking

Street parking is paid, and finding a spot can be very difficult. Near the apartment, sometimes, we’ve found it easier to park here:

  • Via Simone Simoni
  • Via Angelo Emo, 4 (behind the Metro station)
